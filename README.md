Welcome to AquaAssistAlpha!


AquaAssist uses human-made lesson plans provided in its database in order to provide you with the long term lesson plan you need to teach your classes!
These plans are provided in pdf format, and are avalible for the following levels:


Swimmer 6
Swimmer 5
Swimmer 3
Swimmer 2
Swimmer 1
Tiny Tots 5
Tiny Tots 4
Tiny Tots 3
Tiny Tots 1
Junior Masters 1
Junior Masters 2

Unlike AquaAssistBeta, AquaAssistAlpha allows you to extract subsections of a long term lesson plan only. (e.g. front crawl/back crawl day)

NOTE: This is the second version. More versions are to come with even more features
